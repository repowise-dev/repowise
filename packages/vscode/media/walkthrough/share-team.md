# Share with your team and connect Claude.ai

Your index lives on this machine. **Repowise: Publish to repowise.dev** runs
`repowise publish`, which asks repowise.dev to index the same repo from GitHub
(nothing is uploaded, and only what you have pushed is published). You then get:

- one link your team can open in any browser, with nothing to install;
- an MCP address to add in Claude.ai or ChatGPT, which cannot reach your laptop;
- an index that stays up to date on every push (Pro).

Public repos publish free on a free account (up to 2, no card). Private repos and
more repos need Pro, free for 10 days with a card.

[See what hosted adds](https://repowise.dev/hosted?src=vscode_walkthrough#team)
